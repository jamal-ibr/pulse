# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One user: Jamal. He uses Jamal OS daily on his laptop and phone as his
private command centre. Context: short, frequent check-ins (morning
brief, logging habits, checking the Pulse pipeline) plus one longer
weekly review session. He is often on the move, so the phone surface
matters as much as the desktop one.

## Product Purpose

A local-first personal operating system: executive assistant, chief of
staff, accountability system, and memory layer. Its prime directive is
to surface what Jamal is avoiding and protect execution on the highest
leverage work (Pulse AI outreach, health, faith anchors, career,
legacy). Success means Jamal does the scary tasks, not that the
dashboard looks busy.

## Brand Personality

Calm mission control. Direct, honest, strategic. The interface should
feel like a quiet operations room: dark, focused, precise, with cyan
instrumentation accents. Inspired by JARVIS-style HUD interfaces but
cleaner and simpler: no noise, no decoration that does not carry state.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards (white cards, soft shadows, hero metrics)
- Gamified productivity apps with confetti and streak fireworks
- Sci-fi cosplay: scanlines, fake terminals, unreadable glow everywhere
- Anything that flatters. The UI must tell the truth from logged data.

## Design Principles

1. Truth over comfort: avoidance flags and slipping targets are the
   most prominent things on screen, not buried.
2. Instrumentation, not decoration: cyan accents mark state, action,
   and selection only.
3. Legible in two seconds: every screen answers "what matters right
   now" before anything else.
4. Phone-first ergonomics: core logging flows work one-handed on a
   small screen.
5. Local and private by default: nothing in the UI implies cloud sync
   that does not exist.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Body text contrast at or above 4.5:1 on all surfaces
- All motion guarded by prefers-reduced-motion
- Touch targets at least 44px on mobile nav and form controls
- UK English throughout; no em dashes anywhere
